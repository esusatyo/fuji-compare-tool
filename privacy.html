Privacy

This site collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no forms, and no cookies.

Analytics

Anonymous, aggregate page-view statistics are collected with GoatCounter, a cookieless analytics tool. It stores nothing on your device and does not track you across sites.

Local preferences

Your last-visited brand is kept in your browser's localStorage so the site can return you there next visit. This never leaves your device; clearing site data removes it.

Outbound links

Buy buttons lead to the Amazon marketplace for your selected currency, and spec sources link to manufacturer and review sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies that apply once you leave this one.
