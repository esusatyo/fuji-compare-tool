About

Camera Compare is a side-by-side comparison tool for mirrorless cameras and lenses from Canon, Fujifilm, Nikon, Panasonic and Sony — built for photographers deciding what to buy next.

How the data is sourced

Every camera and lens entry is verified against at least two reputable sources — the manufacturer's official specification page plus a major retailer or review site — before it is added. Prices are approximate manufacturer list prices (RRP) in seven currencies and are re-audited periodically; the footer of every page shows the date the data was last verified. If a spec can't be reliably sourced, it is left out rather than guessed.

Spotted an error? Please open an issue on the GitHub repository.

Buy links

Buy buttons link to the Amazon marketplace matching your selected currency. They are plain links — this site currently earns nothing from them.
