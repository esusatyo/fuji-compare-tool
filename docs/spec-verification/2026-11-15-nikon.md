Battery uses the **Energy saving OFF** figures (the stricter ones, matching the
Fujifilm "Normal, not Economy" ruling). z9, z8, zf and z7-ii list only video minutes on
Nikon USA, so they are left as they are.

### Held: burst split
Nikon's continuous rates are split into regular Continuous H (extended), e.g. Z6III
14 fps mechanical / 20 fps electronic, and **high-speed frame capture** (C30/C60/C120),
which is JPEG-only and partly DX-crop. Read literally, the owner's rule ("fastest
electronic rate with the crop noted") would put 120 fps in the Electronic row.
Proposed: exclude high-speed frame capture. Waiting on the owner; no Nikon camera is
converted yet.

### Not checked
IBIS stops (not on the global pages), burst, subject detection.
