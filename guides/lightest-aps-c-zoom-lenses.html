The lightest APS-C zoom lenses, ranked

A zoom you forget is in the bag. Here's who makes one.

By Compare Camera Specs · Updated 26 November 2026

The lightest APS-C zoom lens you can buy new is the Sony E 16-50mm F3.5-5.6 PZ OSS II, at 107 g. That's lighter than most phones. Fujifilm, Canon and Nikon are all within 28 g of it.

Which APS-C zooms are lightest overall?

Here are the ten lightest APS-C zooms on sale, colored by mount.

Fujifilm and Canon each place three. Half of the ten are power zooms, with PZ in the name: a motor does the zooming, so the barrel can tuck away when the camera's off.

What do you give up for a light zoom?

Speed, mostly. Every mount's winner starts at f/3.5 or slower, and gets darker as you zoom in.

Want f/2.8 all the way through? The lightest APS-C option we list is the Sigma 10-18mm f/2.8 DC DN for Fujifilm X, at 250 g. That's more than twice the weight of the E 16-50mm.

What I'd put on my X-S20

Full disclosure: I shoot with a Fujifilm X-S20, and I really enjoy it. My zoom of choice on it is the XC 15-45mm, at 135 g.

Fujifilm's new XC 13-33mm is 10 g lighter, if you're counting. Compare the three lightest X-mount zooms, or weigh my X-S20 and XC 15-45mm kit (626 g all in).

How we weighed them

Weights are each maker's published figure for the lens. We count every APS-C zoom on sale in our database, 75 in all, from first-party and third-party makers. A lens made for two mounts counts once for each.

Full-frame lenses also fit Sony E, Canon RF and Nikon Z APS-C bodies, with a crop, but this ranking covers lenses made for APS-C. Discontinued lenses and lenses that are announced but not on sale yet are left out.
