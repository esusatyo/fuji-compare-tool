## Excluded (with reason)

- **S system** (S2, S Typ 006/007, S3) — medium-format DSLRs; mirrorless-only.
- **Film M** (M6, M-A, MP, M7…) — not digital.
- **Digilux 2 / Digilux 3** — pre-floor; Digilux 3 is a Four Thirds DSLR.
- **Limited/special editions** (M9 Titanium, M10-R Black Paint, Q2 Reporter/
  Ghost, M11 "100 Years", Metal Gray colourways…) — cosmetic, not distinct
  cameras.
- **Rumoured only** as of 2026-09-14 (Leica Rumors, 2026-09-02): Q3 43
  Monochrom, M11-R, M12, SL3 Reporter, medium-format mirrorless. Re-check at
  task 8.2 — anything announced before ship gets a task.
