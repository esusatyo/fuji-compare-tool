# Leica lenses — enumeration (task 1.2, 2026-09-14)

Scope (task 1.4): **current** M and SL lenses; **all** TL lenses. Enumeration
grade — existence, line, current status. Specs, prices and exact years are
sourced at entry (group 6). ⚠ = status or year needs confirming at entry.

`year` convention (design §5, revised here): release year of the **version on
sale**, not the year its optical formula first appeared.

All entries: `manufacturer: 'Leica'`. M lenses: `afType: 'Manual'`, `ois: false`.

## Counts

## Year floor (design §5)

Under the version-year convention, the oldest current M version is likely in
the late 1990s (APO-Summicron-M 90, APO-Telyt-M 135, Summicron-M 35 ASPH.).
Task 4.3 sets the lens floor from the oldest `year` actually entered, not from
this estimate.
