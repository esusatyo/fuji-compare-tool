# NIKKOR Z-Mount Lens Reference

Comprehensive enumeration of Nikon's first-party NIKKOR Z lenses, built from the
Wikipedia "List of Nikon Z-mount lenses" spine, cross-checked against Nikon USA /
imaging.nikon.com official pages and retailers (B&H, Amazon, Roberts) for pricing.
**48 lenses** included.

Notation: focal length and aperture in standard photographic form. DX format crop
= 1.5×. Prices in USD. "Launch" = original MSRP at announcement; "Street" =
approximate current street/retail price (June 2026). Items marked **UNCONFIRMED**
could not be verified from two sources and should ship as `priceIncomplete`.

## Key notes & caveats

- Sources cross-checked: Wikipedia "List of Nikon Z-mount lenses" (spine for
  specs/optical formulas/weights), Nikon USA + imaging.nikon.com, and
  B&H/Amazon/Roberts/DPReview/PetaPixel (pricing).
- Built-in teleconverters: 400/2.8 TC and 600/4 TC have integrated 1.4× TCs.
- Weather sealing: all S-Line + most recent zooms sealed; compact primes
  (26, 28, 35/1.4, 40, 50/1.4) and DX lenses only mount-gasket / minimal — verify
  per-lens before publishing as a hard spec.
- **Unconfirmed pricing (ship `priceIncomplete`):** `z-50mm-f1-4`,
  `z-dx-16-50mm-f2-8-vr`, `z-dx-35mm-f1-7-mc`.
- **Recency flags:** 24-70/2.8 S II, 70-200/2.8 VR S II, DX 16-50/2.8 VR, DX
  35/1.7 MC are 2025–2026 releases — double-check release dates/pricing.
