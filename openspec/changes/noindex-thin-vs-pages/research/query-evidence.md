- **Filter `page contains /vs/`:** 497 impressions, 5 clicks, average position 33.1, 103 visible queries.
  - **Every one of the 103 names a specific comparison** (e.g. "lumix l10 vs s9", "canon r50 vs r50v"), or one of its models.

So an impression on a vs-page means someone searched for that comparison; it isn't Google trying the page for a loose query. What varies is how much demand there is: one impression in 10 weeks versus many phrasings searched repeatedly.
