## Why

**Status: proposal only. Nothing is built until the owner approves the design and answers the
open questions below.**

Searches with spec or buying intent are mostly about one model: "X-T5 weight", "Zf dimensions",
"A7 V specs". The site has no page for one camera. Its specs live only inside the JavaScript
brand tool and inside pairwise vs-pages, so a search for a single model has nothing on this
site to match, however good the data is. Of the page types the site lacks, this is the
biggest gap (SEO plan, 2026-11-26).

The data for such a page already exists for every current camera: all 98 current cameras carry
`specSources` citations, dimensions and weight (drawn to scale by `size.js`), prices in seven
currencies, a `mount` with a mount page, and successor/predecessor links derivable from the
vs-page pairing rules.

The risk is scaled, templated content: ~240 vs-pages already taught us that many near-identical
generated pages are a site-quality liability, not free traffic. So this proposal starts with a
small pilot and widens only on evidence.

## What Changes

- **One static spec page per camera**, e.g. `/fujifilm/x-t5`, generated by
  `scripts/generate-seo.js` from brand data. No hand-written text.
  - Headline specs (the `SITE_CONFIG.keySpecs` list, via `key-specs.js`) and the full vs-page
    spec rows, each section with the camera's existing `specSources` citations.
  - Dimensions and weight with a `size.js` drawing next to a credit card.
  - List prices in all seven currencies (USD-only when that's all the data has, as elsewhere).
  - Release year, and links to its predecessor/successor's page when one exists.
  - Its mount, linking the mount page, with the count of lenses that fit (from the mount model).
  - Links to every existing vs-page and size page that includes the camera.
  - A "Compare with…" call to action into the tool, preselected (`../#cameras=<slug>`).
  - `Product` + `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD (Home → brand → camera), validated by the
    structured-data test. No ratings or reviews.
- **Title pattern**: "Fujifilm X-T5 Specs, Size & Weight".
- **Pilot first** (recommended: ~18 pages; see design), indexable and in the sitemap. Other
  current cameras are either generated `noindex` or not generated (owner's choice).
  Discontinued cameras: `noindex` or skipped (owner's choice).
- **Internal links** once the pilot ships: the brand at-a-glance table's camera names, vs-pages
  (product cards), mount pages and (if PR #99 lands) the finder pages link to the camera page.
- **Measurement**: impressions and clicks on model-name queries in Google Search Console and Bing,
  6–8 weeks after the pilot ships, logged in `docs/seo-experiments.md`.
- **Optional second part, wording approved first**: a short data-built "Which should you pick?"
  paragraph on vs-pages (lighter for travel, more pixels, longer battery, better IBIS), using
  the `KeySpecs` verdict rules. The owner approves the sentence templates before anything is
  generated, because templated prose is the scaled-content risk in its purest form.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `camera-pages`: per-camera static spec pages, their indexability, and the links to them.

## Impact

- `scripts/generate-seo.js` (page template, sitemap, stale-file removal for `<brand>/<slug>.html`,
  links from brand tables, vs-pages and mount pages), `engine.css` (page styles),
  share cards (`og/<brand>/<slug>.png`, rendered by `scripts/render-share-images.js`).
- `analytics.js` event grammar and `privacy.html` for new link events.
- Tests: a new `tests/data/camera-pages.test.js`; the orphan-link, sitemap, share-image,
  static-`<h1>` and structured-data tests cover the new pages automatically.
- `CLAUDE.md` (Architecture: camera pages).
- No data changes.

## Open questions for the owner

1. **URL shape.** `/<brand>/<slug>` (recommended) does not clash with the brand tool's `#`
   routing, which lives in the fragment of `/<brand>/`. It would clash only with a camera slug
   named `vs`, `size`, `data` or `index`; none exists, and a test would guard it.
   Alternatives: `/<brand>/camera/<slug>` or `/camera/<brand>-<slug>`.
2. **Pilot size and rule.** (a) current cameras in at least two pinned vs-pages (18 today);
   (b) every current camera in any indexable vs-page (51); (c) all 98 current cameras.
3. **Non-pilot cameras**: generate with `noindex` (visitors can still reach them from links), or
   don't generate them until they're in scope?
4. **Discontinued cameras**: `noindex`, or skip?
5. **Lenses**: same treatment later, or never?
6. **Finder pages (PR #99)**: should they link to camera pages once both exist?
7. **"Which should you pick?" paragraph**: build it at all? If so, the owner writes or approves
   the sentence templates first.
